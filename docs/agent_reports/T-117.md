## 1. Problem & Context
At high forward velocities (particularly on the `Race Car` and on steep downhill slopes), vehicle chassis previously experienced zero horizontal aerodynamic resistance, leading to unrealistic terminal velocity and artificial coasting behavior.

---

## 2. Technical Implementation

### A. Mathematical Formulation
In `PlayerCar::Update(float dt)`, aerodynamic drag force is applied directly to the chassis center of mass opposing the linear velocity vector $\vec{v}$:
$$\vec{F}_{\text{drag}} = -c_{\text{drag}} \cdot \|\vec{v}\| \cdot \vec{v}$$

To prevent numerical instability or runaway forces at extreme anomalous speeds:
- Force magnitude: $\|\vec{F}_{\text{drag}}\| = c_{\text{drag}} \cdot \|\vec{v}\|^2$
- Upper bound clamp: $\min(\|\vec{F}_{\text{drag}}\|, M_{\text{chassis}} \times 100.0\text{ m/s}^2)$
- Applied force: $\vec{F}_{\text{drag}} = \left(-\frac{\|\vec{F}_{\text{drag}}\|}{\|\vec{v}\|}\right) \cdot \vec{v}$
