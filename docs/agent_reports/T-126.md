## 2. Dragster Wheel Asymmetry
In accordance with original 1.30.0 physics:
- Dragster features a tiny front guide wheel and massive rear drive slick:
  - `front_wheel_radius = 0.28m`
  - `rear_wheel_radius = 0.58m`
  - Ratio: $0.58 / 0.28 = 2.07\times > 1.40\times$.
- `rear_wheel_texture = "assets/car-dragster-rear-tire.png"`
- `front_wheel_texture = "assets/Tire.png"`
- The chassis center of mass is shifted forward to balance high wheelie torque under downforce.
