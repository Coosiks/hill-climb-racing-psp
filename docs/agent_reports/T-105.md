# Agent Report: T-105 (physics) — In-Air Sensor Filtering & Contact Validation

## 1. Goal & Context
In Hill Climb Racing, jumping through arcs of coins or fuel cans must not disable air pitch control or reset flip rotation accumulators.
Previously, `PlayerCar::IsInAir()` simply checked `contact->IsTouching()` on wheel contact edges. Because Box2D creates and marks contacts as touching even for sensor fixtures (`b2Fixture::IsSensor()`), overlapping a coin sensor counted as ground contact:
- `IsInAir()` returned `false`.
- `mAirTime` was immediately reset to `0.0f`.
- `mAirRotation` was reset to `0.0f`, wiping out progress toward backflips/frontflips.
- Pitch control (`AIR_PITCH_TORQUE`) was deactivated (requires `mAirTime > 0.08f`).

## 2. Changes Made
1. **`src/physics/PlayerCar.cpp`:**
   - Implemented `IsGroundContact(b2ContactEdge* ce, b2Body* wheel)` helper function:
     - Verifies `ce->contact && ce->contact->IsTouching()`.
     - Validates that NEITHER fixture is a sensor (`!fA->IsSensor() && !fB->IsSensor()`).
     - Extracts the opposing fixture (`fA == wheel ? fB : fA`) and ensures its collision category contains `CAT_TERRAIN` (`other->GetFilterData().categoryBits & CAT_TERRAIN`).
   - Refactored `PlayerCar::IsInAir()` to iterate wheel contacts and immediately return `false` on the first valid ground contact, or `true` if all contacts are sensors/non-terrain.
2. **`CMakeLists.txt` & `.github/workflows/build.yml`:**
   - Added automated regression test `test_air_sensors` (`tools/test_air_sensors.cpp`).
   - Integrated `./build-pc/test_air_sensors` into the CI test matrix.

## 3. Regression Test Results (`test_air_sensors`)
A car was launched on an airborne trajectory ($v_x = 12.0\text{ m/s}, v_y = 3.5\text{ m/s}$) through a parabolic arc of 10 coin sensors (`CAT_ITEM`, `isSensor = true`, $R = 0.35\text{ m}$) followed by a terrain landing strip at $X \ge 15\text{ m}$:
- **Coin sensor contact duration:** 28 frames of continuous/overlapping contact during flight.
- **Air time preservation:** `mAirTime` was never reset to `0.0f` during sensor contacts and continued accumulating monotonically up to $1.533\text{ s}$.
- **Air control availability:** Maintained throughout the coin arc without interruption.
- **Touchdown validation:** Upon contacting actual ground (`CAT_TERRAIN`) at step 91, `IsInAir()` transitioned to `false` and `mAirTime` reset to `0.0f`.
- **Status:** **PASS**.
