## 2. Terrain Generation & Mathematical Modeling

### 2.1 Pure `double` Precision Generators
All new stage height functions in `src/physics/Terrain.cpp` use standard library `double` math functions (`sin`, `cos`) with double literals (`0.05`, `1.5`, `12.0`) to avoid trigonometric rounding divergence between x86_64 glibc and MIPS newlib soft-float:
- **Seasons (Index 18):** Gentle seasonal rolling hills with multi-frequency seasonal undulating waves.
- **Bootcamp (Index 19):** Rugged training terrain featuring steep assault trenches, obstacle humps, and sharp crests.
- **Xmas (Index 20):** Snowdrift mounds with rolling winter hills and pine slope undulations.
- **Alien Planet (Index 21):** Extraterrestrial jagged craters, low-frequency planetary swells, and steep extraterrestrial ridges.
- **Arctic Cave (Index 22):** Cavernous sub-zero floor contours paired with ceiling generation (`has_ceiling = 1`), enforcing $\ge 4.5$m vertical clearance to prevent vehicle entrapment while posing driver head collision risk.
- **Ragnarok (Index 23):** Mythological volcanic wasteland with apocalyptic terrain ridges and lava mud pools (`has_mud_pools = 1`, `mud_damping = 2.2`).

### 2.2 Stage Flags & Mechanics
- `has_ceiling = 1`: Enabled for `cave` (5) and `arcticcave` (22).
- `has_mud_pools = 1`: Enabled for `mudpool` (9), `volcano` (10), `junkyard` (15), and `ragnarok` (23).
- `has_bridges = 1`: Enabled for `forest` (7), `mountain` (8), `rooftops` (14), and `construction` (16).
- `is_night = 1`: Strictly isolated to `night` (13).
