# Agent Report: T-106 (physics) — Item Lifecycle & Post-Step Deferred Destruction

## 1. Goal & Context
In Hill Climb Racing, collectibles (coins and fuel canisters) are placed along the stage.
Previously:
1. `PhysicsWorld::BeginContact` set `itemFixture->SetUserData((void*)1)` upon item collection. On subsequent frames, `(uintptr_t)itemFixture->GetUserData()` evaluated `1 > 0`, causing repeated coin additions (`OnCoinCollected(1)`) indefinitely as long as the car was touching the item.
2. Item bodies were never destroyed inside `BeginContact` (forbidden by Box2D during contact callbacks) and lacked an immediate post-step destruction queue, leaking dynamic bodies in the Box2D world.

## 2. Root Cause & Solution Architecture
1. **Deferred Destruction Queue (Ring Buffer):**
   - Implemented a zero-allocation circular ring-buffer `mDestroyQueue[DEFERRED_QUEUE_CAPACITY]` (capacity 64) inside `Terrain`.
   - When an item is touched in `PhysicsWorld::BeginContact`:
     - Checks sentinel: `if (itemType == 0) return;` (prevents duplicate triggers).
     - Checks item flag: `if (item->collected) return;`
     - Sets fixture sentinel `itemFixture->SetUserData((void*)0)` immediately so any subsequent contact points in the same step are rejected.
     - Sets `item->collected = true`.
     - Calls listener callback: `OnCoinCollected(value)` or `OnFuelCollected()` exactly once.
     - Enqueues body pointer: `mTerrain->EnqueueDestroy(itemBody)`.
2. **Post-Step Deferred Processing (`Terrain::ProcessDeferredDestroy`):**
   - Called immediately following `world.Step()` in `Game::UpdatePlaying()`:
     ```cpp
     mPhysics->Step(FIXED_TIMESTEP);
     b2Vec2 pos = mCar->GetPosition();
     mTerrain->ProcessDeferredDestroy(pos.x);
     mCar->Update(FIXED_TIMESTEP);
     mTerrain->Update(pos.x);
     ```
   - Dequeues queued bodies and destroys them via `mWorld->DestroyBody(body)`.
   - Despawns uncollected items far behind the vehicle: items with $x < carX - 40.0\text{ m}$ are destroyed and removed from `mItems`.
   - Updates `body->SetUserData(&mItems[i])` when array elements are swapped during compaction to maintain valid references.

## 3. Regression Test Verification (`tools/test_items.cpp`)
All three sub-tests were executed under PC build and verified:

### Sub-test A: Single Collection & Duplicate Contact Immunity
- Spawned coin (value 500) at $(5.0, 2.0)$ and fuel canister at $(15.0, 2.0)$.
- Spawned collector body with multi-fixture overlap (`CAT_CHASSIS` and `CAT_WHEEL`).
- Step 1: `coinCalls = 1`, `coinTotal = 500`.
- Processed deferred destroy.
- 10 subsequent simulation steps overlapping same position: `coinCalls = 1`, `coinTotal = 500` (0 duplicate increments).
- Touching fuel canister: `fuelCalls = 1`. 10 subsequent steps: `fuelCalls = 1` (0 duplicate increments).
- **Result:** **PASS**.

### Sub-test B: 10-Minute Continuous Simulated Drive
- Simulated $10\text{ minutes} = 600.0\text{ s} = 36{,}000\text{ steps}$ at 60 Hz (`TIMESTEP = 1/60s`).
- Car driven with active cruise control along `STAGE_COUNTRYSIDE`.
- Telemetry sampled every 5.0 s (every 300 steps) and written to `items_10min_telemetry.csv`:
  - Initial world body count: 5 (chassis, 2 wheels, driver head, terrain).
  - Maximum body count observed during entire 10-minute run: 11 bodies.
  - Minimum body count observed: 5 bodies.
  - Body count is strictly bounded in $[5, 11]$ (budget threshold $\le 25$).
- **Result:** **PASS** (zero Box2D body memory leak over 36,000 steps).

### Sub-test C: Despawn of Items Behind $carX - 40\text{ m}$
- Spawned item 1 at $X = 10.0\text{ m}$ and item 2 at $X = 70.0\text{ m}$.
- Positioned vehicle at $X = 45.0\text{ m}$ ($carX - 40 = 5.0\text{ m} < 10.0\text{ m}$):
  - Item 1 was preserved (not despawned).
- Advanced vehicle to $X = 55.0\text{ m}$ ($carX - 40 = 15.0\text{ m} > 10.0\text{ m}$):
  - Item 1 was destroyed and removed.
  - Item 2 at $X = 70.0\text{ m}$ remained active.
- **Result:** **PASS**.
