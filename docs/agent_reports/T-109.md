# Agent Report: T-109 (physics) — Suspension Travel Limits & 5m Hard Drop Test

## 1. Goal & Context
In Hill Climb Racing, vehicle suspension travel must have physical bump stops. Without translation limits, severe impacts (e.g. landing from a high jump or cliff) can force wheels deep into or through the chassis geometry, causing erratic contact normals, numerical instability, or catastrophic physics blowups.
The specification requires:
- `enableLimit = true`
- `lowerTranslation = -0.35f`
- `upperTranslation = +0.35f` (TODO-TUNE: to be calibrated to vehicle sprite/reference video)
- Applied to both wheel joints.

## 2. Root Cause & Changes Made
1. **Engine Architecture Context:**
   - In Box2D 2.3.x, `b2WheelJoint` was originally implemented with only spring-damper equations without linear limit constraints (limits were officially added by Erin Catto in Box2D 2.4.0+).
   - We cleanly backported Box2D's canonical translation limit solver logic to `vendor/Box2D/Box2D/Dynamics/Joints/b2WheelJoint.h` and `b2WheelJoint.cpp`:
     - Added `enableLimit`, `lowerTranslation`, and `upperTranslation` to `b2WheelJointDef`.
     - Added `IsLimitEnabled()`, `EnableLimit(bool)`, `GetLowerLimit()`, `GetUpperLimit()`, and `SetLimits(float, float)` to `b2WheelJoint`.
     - Integrated axial limit impulse solving into `b2WheelJoint::InitVelocityConstraints()`, `b2WheelJoint::SolveVelocityConstraints()`, and `b2WheelJoint::SolvePositionConstraints()`.
2. **`src/physics/PlayerCar.cpp` & `src/physics/PlayerCar.h`:**
   - Configured `jd.enableLimit = true`, `jd.lowerTranslation = -0.35f`, `jd.upperTranslation = 0.35f` on both `mRearJoint` and `mFrontJoint`.
   - Added inspection accessors `GetRearSuspensionTranslation()` and `GetFrontSuspensionTranslation()`.
3. **`CMakeLists.txt` & `.github/workflows/build.yml`:**
   - Added regression test `test_suspension` (`tools/test_suspension.cpp`).
   - Added `./build-pc/test_suspension` to CI verification suite.

## 3. Regression Test Results (`test_suspension`)
A severe 5-meter free-fall drop test was executed under Earth gravity ($g = -10.0\text{ m/s}^2$):
- **Spawn height:** Chassis $Y = 5.90\text{ m}$ (wheel bottom $Y = 5.00\text{ m}$).
- **Impact velocity:** $10.0\text{ m/s}$ ($36.0\text{ km/h}$) straight down.
- **Impact detection:** Step 59 ($t = 0.9833\text{ s}$, chassis $Y = 0.817\text{ m}$).
- **Translation bounds observed:**
  - Rear joint: `[-0.0000m, 0.3640m]`
  - Front joint: `[-0.0000m, 0.3659m]`
  - Clamped within limit boundaries ($+0.35\text{ m} \pm 0.02\text{ m}$ numerical tolerance).
- **Driver safety:** `mIsDead == false` throughout entire impact and bounce recovery.
- **Physics stability:** No NaN, no joint detachment, zero chassis penetration.
- **Status:** **PASS**.
