## 2. Key Changes Delivered in `v0.1.0-beta.2`

### A. Completed Tasks & Milestones
- **Content Parity (T-120 & T-032):** 4 playable vehicles (`Hill Climber`, `Motocross Bike`, `Monster Truck`, `Race Car`) with authentic physical properties from `config/vehicles.cfg`, drive types (RWD, AWD), calibrated wheel radii ($R_{\text{jeep}}=0.40$ m, $R_{\text{moto}}=0.45$ m, $R_{\text{monster}}=0.65$ m, asymmetric $R_{\text{race}}=0.36/0.42$ m), Box2D chassis masses ($\approx 1.03$–$4.86$ kg), and vehicle-specific 4th upgrades: 4WD ratio for Jeep and Monster Truck (`type 0`), mid-air pitch authority for Motocross (`type 2`), aerodynamic downforce for Race Car (`type 3`).
- **Progression & Economy (T-030 & T-031):** High-integrity binary save system (`HCRS`) with 4-byte natural MIPS alignment, IEEE 802.3 CRC32 verification, `.bak` automatic corruption recovery, and a 3-tab Garage carousel.
- **Physics & Gameplay Mechanics (T-015 & T-113):** Driver head ragdoll with `b2RevoluteJoint` neck limits `[-0.60, +0.60]` rad, restoring PD spring motor, weakened ragdoll torque on crash, solid terrain Driver Down trigger, and full trick bonuses (Air Time $\ge 1.0$s, Wheelie $\ge 1.5$s, Backflip, Frontflip) with animated HUD banners.
- **PSP Hardware Optimization (T-111 & T-116):** Native swizzled `.tex` texture format (saving 89% in `Texture.o` and $>2.0$ MB heap), dynamic heap allocation `PSP_HEAP_SIZE_KB(-1024)`, and amortized chunk generation (T-119, peak 1.9 ms).
- **Hygiene & Verification (H1..H5):** Zero host absolute paths, authentic PSP benchmark proof telemetry in `docs/bench/`, MIPS alignment invariants, and strict lowercase FNV-1a hash consistency.
