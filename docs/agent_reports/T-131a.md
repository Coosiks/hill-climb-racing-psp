## 2. Physical & Box2D Modeling

### 2.1 Elongated Wheelbase & Mass Distribution
To capture the authentic feel of a heavy railway locomotive:
- **Wheelbase:** Set to $3.80$ m (`rear_wheel_offset = -1.90, -0.45`, `front_wheel_offset = 1.90, -0.45`), strictly exceeding the Tourist Bus ($3.40$ m).

- **Drive Type:** All-Wheel Drive (`drive_type = 2`), high motor torque ($24.0$ N$\cdot$m).
- **Angular Damping:** Base damping elevated to $0.60$ in `PlayerCar.cpp` for locomotive inertia.
