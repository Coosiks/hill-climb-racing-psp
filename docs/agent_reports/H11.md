## 3. Night Mode Layer Ordering and Camera Projection

### 3.1 Camera Tracking & World-to-Screen Projection
In `src/graphics/Renderer2D.h` and `Renderer2D.cpp`:
- `SetupCamera` records `sCameraX = camX; sCameraY = camY; sCameraZoom = zoom;`.
- `WorldToScreen` projects Box2D world meters to screen pixels:
  $$sx = \text{PSP\_SCREEN\_WIDTH} \cdot 0.5\text{f} + (wx - sCameraX) \cdot (\text{PTM\_RATIO} \cdot sCameraZoom)$$
  $$sy = \text{PSP\_SCREEN\_HEIGHT} \cdot 0.5\text{f} - (wy - sCameraY) \cdot (\text{PTM\_RATIO} \cdot sCameraZoom)$$
- The negative sign in $sy$ accounts for the vertical inversion between Box2D world space (+Y up) and PSP/OpenGL screen space (+Y down from top-left $(0, 0)$).

### 3.2 Layer Ordering & Additive Blending
In `src/core/Game.cpp` (`RenderPlaying()`):
1. **First:** Fullscreen ambient darkening `DrawFilledRect(0, 0, 480, 272, 0xB0080604)` is drawn in screen space.
2. **Second:** `DrawLightCone` is drawn on top with additive blending (`GU_ADD, GU_SRC_ALPHA, GU_ONE` on PSP, `glBlendFunc(GL_SRC_ALPHA, GL_ONE)` on PC OpenGL).
3. **Third:** HUD is rendered in screen space on top of the illuminated scene.

### 3.3 Honest Metrics
Colored untextured primitives bypass the textured sprite quad batcher (`sBatchVertices`), producing 2 draw calls (1 for darkening rect, 1 for headlight cone) and 0 batched sprite quads (`sBatchedQuadCount = 0`). The errata in `docs/agent_reports/T-125.md` were corrected.
